# BirdGlass webpage direction

Audience: property and facilities teams preparing measured glass retrofits. Primary job: move from a window photo and measurements to a readable treatment and selected building budget. This is a browser webpage, with no app shell or desktop-style side navigation.

## Tokens and composition

- Ink: deep marine #18344e.
- Action: architectural blue #2864df.
- Canvas: cool mist #f5f8fc.
- Surface: white #ffffff.
- Line: blue-grey #dce5ef.
- Verified: restrained botanical green #27725c; pending review uses readable amber.
- Type: locally bundled Manrope variable font for both headings and body; 52 px desktop hero, 30 px section headings, 14–15 px readable body, tabular numerals for quantities and costs.
- Composition: centred 1400 px content, wide photographic workspace, an adjacent practical measurement form, and continuous results and planning sections below. Left alignment supports scanning and comparing measurements.

## Review against the brief

The earlier sidebar and repeated tiny all-capital labels made the site resemble a dense developer dashboard. Replace the sidebar with a horizontal, photo-led window collection; retain one clear primary action per section. Use a custom bird-and-glass drawing as the single decorative moment, with the real photo doing the main visual work. Blue is drawn from reflected sky and architectural glazing; green is reserved for verified status rather than colouring every surface. Avoid a generic cream-and-serif landing page, excessive rounded tiles and artificial live-effectiveness claims.

Keep functional inputs, accessibility labels, IDs/revisions, geometry and cost logic intact. Support visible keyboard focus, reduced motion, wrapping long names, and narrow screens. Review actual rendered screenshots before delivery. Phone-width checks are browser layout checks, not hardware performance evidence.
